Analytical Finding

Car occupants (drivers and passengers) represent nearly half of all admissions, followed by motorcyclists and pedal cyclists. Switching to Bed Days reveals disproportionate inpatient stays among older Australians (65–74 and 75+ average 6.5+ bed days per crash) and motorcyclists due to high-energy orthopaedic trauma.

How to Use CrashMetrics

  • Use Left/Right Arrow to move through navigation links or chart data. Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move through all controls; use arrow keys to adjust a focused year slider.
  • Use the playback button to animate years; select it again to pause.
  • Choose a state from the dropdown or select a heatmap cell to inspect its trajectory.
  • For the heatmap, use all four arrow keys to move by year and state. Elsewhere, use Left/Right Arrow to move through chart data; line-chart points follow year order. Press Enter or Space on a heatmap cell to select a state.
  • Focus or point to chart marks for details. Press Esc to dismiss a tooltip.
  • Hatched n.p. values are not published to protect privacy; they are not zero.
